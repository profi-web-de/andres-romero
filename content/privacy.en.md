# Privacy policy

## Hosting

This site is served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub Inc.). Visiting it processes
server logs containing the IP address and browser data.

## Fonts

Fonts are **self-hosted** and loaded from this server. There is no connection
to Google Fonts.

## Contact form

The contact form is sent through **formsubmit.co**. Name, email address and
message are transmitted to that service, which forwards them to the inbox.

## Videos

Embedded videos are loaded from **YouTube** (Google Ireland Ltd.) when the
relevant page is opened.

## Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is **not active** at present. Once enabled, it will run only
with prior consent via the banner; without consent nothing is loaded. Ad
storage remains disabled at all times.
