Andrés Romero G. is a musician, guitarist, composer and music teacher. He holds a bachelor's degree in Instrumental Pedagogy from the Universidad Pedagógica Nacional in Colombia.

His training spans jazz, popular music, rock and Latin American music, with particular focus on Afro-Colombian, Afro-Cuban and Brazilian traditions. He works both in academic settings and in practice: with several bands and as a soloist.

He currently lives in Erfurt, Thuringia.
